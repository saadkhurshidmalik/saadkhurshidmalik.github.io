Ajzal Benaa

A seamless, bilingual E-Commerce solution built with Flutter. Efficiently serving English and Arabic markets with real-time Right-to-Left (RTL) localization.

Project Overview

Ajzal Benaa provides a high-performance mobile shopping experience tailored for retail in the Middle East. Developed entirely using Flutter, the platform delivers native performance across Android and iOS while utilizing a sophisticated internationalization engine to provide fluid, automatic switching between English and Arabic layouts, including complete RTL adaptation.

Flutter Dart REST APIs i18n RTL Engine Android / iOS GitLab CI/CD
